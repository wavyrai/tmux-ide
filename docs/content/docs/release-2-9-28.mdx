`2.9.28` is a CLI/daemon release about connections that come back by
themselves. Panes with a long scrollback open on stock tmux 3.8 and on the
bundled tmux, sessions with floating panes open, a pane the daemon holds back
says why, and one bad pane no longer closes a whole session. It adds no
commands. Native Mac alpha 0.1.22 comes with it: keys typed during a drop are
held or announced, and the window recovers from dropped links, sleep, slow SSH
and remote daemon restarts without a refresh.

## Connections recover by themselves

In the Mac app 0.1.22:

- A link that gives no answer for 5 s is replaced, without waiting for the
  network to give up. When the Mac wakes or the network changes, every open
  link is checked at once.
- From 2 s into a drop, the pane shows "Reconnecting…" with a "Retry now"
  button.
- Over SSH, a slow link no longer ends in "Couldn't reconnect. Refresh
  sessions to try again." A window opened for one SSH destination reopens its
  session after the remote daemon restarts.
- A saved SSH machine's Home loads again by itself after a failed load, as the
  local one does. A machine that answers as a different machine stops with "A
  different machine answered at this address. Check the machine, then refresh
  sessions."
- Switching to another app and back no longer reconnects Home or the sidebar,
  and the agent list stays where it was.
- A focus change that meets a dead link no longer stops the window's
  terminals. If the terminal part of the window stops anyway, it restarts on
  its own and reopens your session, showing "Connection lost. Reconnecting…"
  meanwhile.

Before the release, a soak ran on main for 4 legs of 5 hours, local and over
SSH, with 2,761 injected faults. No session stayed stuck, none needed a refresh,
the terminal part of the window never stopped, and the app did not crash.

## Typed keys are never lost without a word

Keys typed while a session reconnects are held, up to 512 keys for 5 s, and
then sent once, in order, to the same pane. If that is not certain, none of
them are sent, and the pane says "N keys not sent. Type them again when the
connection is back."

Keys that could not be sent for another reason are announced once you are
back on that session, until you type again:

- Typed just as the window lost focus: "Keys typed as the window lost focus
  were not sent. Type them again."
- Typed as the session's connection was replaced, for example when another
  client added a pane: "Keys typed as the terminal reconnected were not sent.
  Type them again."

The rest of a paste cut short counts the same way. Keys are never sent late or
twice.

## Long scrollbacks open

- On stock tmux 3.8, a pane with a long history opens. Before, a pane with a
  full default history of 2,000 lines could fail to open, and every retry
  failed the same way.
- With the bundled tmux, a pane larger than 1,000,000 cells opens. Its screen
  and newest history arrive first, and older lines follow in pieces.
- The daemon keeps up to 10,000 lines of scrollback per pane. tmux keeps the
  rest of its history as before.

## A held pane says why

A pane that failed to open twice in a row is held back for a while instead of
being tried every 2 s. The Mac app now says so, with a countdown:

- "Can't open this session: a pane's scrollback is too long to send. Clear it
  with tmux clear-history, or wait. Trying again in 12 s."
- "Can't open this session: tmux could not send a pane twice in a row. Trying
  again in 12 s."

The footer and the window title say "Can't open this session" instead of
counting attempts. "Retry now" tries at once and reads "Trying…" while it
runs. Clearing the pane's history ends the hold.

## Floating panes and large sessions

- A session that holds a floating pane (tmux 3.7 and later) or a modal pane
  (tmux 3.8) opens. Before, it failed with "Session has no attachable terminal
  panes". The floating pane itself is not shown yet.
- One pane that fails to open no longer closes the session. The rest opens,
  and that pane is tried again after 1, 2 and 4 s.
- `tmux-ide app` no longer stays on "connecting" for a session with more than
  24 panes. It says, with your session's numbers: "Session too large to stream
  here: 26 panes, the limit is 24. Move some windows to another session, or
  open it in the Mac app."

## Updating

Update with `tmux-ide update` or rerun the installer. A running older daemon
restarts on 2.9.28 by itself; your tmux sessions keep running. Update remote
machines too: long scrollbacks, floating panes and the held-pane reasons are in
the daemon on the machine that runs tmux. "Retry now" inside a hold needs a
2.9.28 daemon.
